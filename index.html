What it does

A backup you can hold.

Only what is new

Every run copies only what the drive does not already hold. A second backup right after the first copies nothing — no duplicates, no re-copying 40,000 photos to add yesterday’s.

The originals, not re-encodes

Full-resolution HEIC, JPEG and RAW. Original-quality video. Live Photo pairs kept together. Edited and unedited versions both.

Verified, not assumed

Every file is hashed while it is written, then size-checked afterwards. Nothing enters the catalog until it has been verified, and you can re-verify the whole archive whenever you like.

Yours to read without this app

Ordinary folders and files, sorted by date, with a plain-text catalog beside them. Any computer can open it. If Gallerya disappeared tomorrow, your photos would not.

Free up space, safely

A green shield means the archive already holds it.
An orange triangle means it is not backed up yet. Freeing space never means losing a photo.

Works completely offline

There is no server and nothing is uploaded. Gallerya has no idea whether you are online — a backup that needs a signal is not a backup you control.

On the phone

Your archive, not a preview of it.

How it works

Three steps, then it is just a gallery.

  1. Plug in a drive

    A USB-C flash drive, an external SSD, or a memory card reader. Gallerya creates one folder inside whichever folder you pick, and touches nothing else on the drive.

  2. Back up

    It compares your library against the drive and copies what is missing, hashing every file as it goes. You get a figure and an estimate before it starts.

  3. Browse, verify, or free up space

    Your archive as a proper gallery — by year and month, albums preserved, full-screen viewer, video playback with scrubbing. Re-verify it any time, or use Cleanup to delete from your phone what the drive provably holds.
