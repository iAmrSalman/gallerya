What it does

A backup you can hold.

Only what is new

Every run copies only what the drive does not already hold. A second backup right after the first copies nothing — no duplicates, no re-copying 40,000 photos to add yesterday’s.

The originals, not re-encodes

Full-resolution HEIC, JPEG and RAW. Original-quality video. Live Photo pairs kept together. Edited and unedited versions both.

Verified, not assumed

Every file is hashed while it is written, then size-checked afterwards. Nothing enters the catalog until it has been verified, and you can re-verify the whole archive whenever you like.

Yours to read without this app

Ordinary folders and files, sorted by date, with a plain-text catalog beside them. Any computer can open it. If Gallerya disappeared tomorrow, your photos would not.

Free up space, safely

Works completely offline

There is no server and nothing is uploaded. Gallerya has no idea whether you are online — a backup that needs a signal is not a backup you control.

The archive

Everything on the drive, as a gallery.

The archive tab reads the drive, not a thumbnail cache of it. Grouped by year and month, with your albums preserved — the ones you made and the smart ones alike — so what you copied is still organised the way you organised it.

Open anything, full screen

Photos straight from the drive at full resolution. Videos play immediately with a scrubbable bar, mute, and 1×, 1.5× or 2× speed — in portrait or landscape.

Share it, or take it back

Share anything out, or copy it back into Photos. Hold a photo to start selecting and do it to a dozen at once. Deleting from your phone to free space was never the end of it.

Cleanup

Free up space without gambling.

Cleanup finds what is probably worth losing — screen recordings, exact duplicates, similar shots, the large files and long videos eating your storage — and shows you where the space actually went, by year, largest first.

Every item says whether it is safe

A green shield means the archive already holds it, so deleting here cannot lose it.
An orange triangle means it is not backed up yet.

Checked before anything is deleted

By default every archived copy is re-read and compared against its recorded SHA-256 first. One mismatch anywhere cancels the whole deletion. A few seconds, against deleting your only copy of a photo.

Two different jobs, kept separate: deleting from Photos frees space on your phone and leaves the archive untouched. Removing from the archive frees space on the drive and leaves your photo library untouched.

How it works

Three steps, then it is just a gallery.

  1. Plug in a drive

    A USB-C flash drive, an external SSD, or a memory card reader. Gallerya creates one folder inside whichever folder you pick, and touches nothing else on the drive.

  2. Back up

    It compares your library against the drive and copies what is missing, hashing every file as it goes. You get a figure and an estimate before it starts.

  3. Browse, verify, or free up space

    Your archive as a proper gallery — by year and month, albums preserved, full-screen viewer, video playback with scrubbing. Re-verify it any time, or use Cleanup to delete from your phone what the drive provably holds.
